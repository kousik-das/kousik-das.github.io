Welcome

Dr. Kousik Das

Postdoctoral Research Scientist

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at Laboratoire Informatique d'Avignon (LIA), Centre d'Enseignement et de Recherche en Informatique (CERI), Avignon Université, France.

I work in the CORNET research team under Prof. Yezekayel Hayel. My research focuses on Queueing Theory, Polling Systems, Reinforcement Learning, Mean Field Games and Network Optimization.
