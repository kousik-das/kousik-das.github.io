Welcome

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at LIA, CERI, Avignon Université, France, working in the CORNET research team under Prof. Yezekayel Hayel.

My research focuses on Queueing Theory, Polling Systems, Applied Probability, Reinforcement Learning, Markov Decision Processes, Stochastic Games, Mean Field Games, and Network Optimization.
