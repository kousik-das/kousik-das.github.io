Welcome

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at LIA, CERI, Avignon Université, France, working in the CORNET research team under Prof. Yezekayel Hayel.

My research focuses on mathematical modelling, queueing theory, polling systems, reinforcement learning, stochastic games, mean field games, applied probability, and network optimization.
