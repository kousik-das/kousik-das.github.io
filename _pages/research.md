My research lies at the intersection of **Applied Probability**, **Operations Research**, **Stochastic Approximations**, **Reinforcement Learning**, and **Game Theory**. I develop mathematical models and computational algorithms for analyzing complex stochastic systems arising in communication networks, service systems, cyber-security, and intelligent decision-making.

My work combines rigorous probabilistic analysis with algorithmic methods to address challenging problems involving uncertainty, partial information, and dynamic interactions among multiple agents.

---

## Current Research

### Queueing Systems and Polling Models

I investigate the stability, performance analysis, and optimization of queueing systems, with particular emphasis on polling systems under Markovian switching policies, fluid approximations, and matrix-analytic techniques.

---

### Reinforcement Learning and Stochastic Games

My current research focuses on reinforcement learning algorithms for partially observable stochastic games, Bayesian learning, fictitious play, pursuit-evasion games, and decision-making under uncertainty.

---

### Network Optimization

I study optimization problems arising in communication and service networks, including resource allocation, scheduling, cyber deception, and strategic interactions among autonomous agents.

---

## Current Projects

- Stability of polling systems with generalized Markovian switching policies.
- Reinforcement learning for partially observable stochastic games.
- Bayesian fictitious play for pursuit–evasion games.
- Cyber-deception games on multilayer networks.
- Queueing models with batch arrivals and bulk services.

---

## Research Vision

My long-term research goal is to develop mathematically rigorous and computationally efficient methodologies for stochastic decision-making in large-scale networked systems. I am particularly interested in bridging probability theory, optimization, reinforcement learning, and game theory to address emerging challenges in communication networks, intelligent transportation systems, cyber-security, and autonomous multi-agent systems.
