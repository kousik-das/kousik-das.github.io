I am a **Postdoctoral Research Scientist** at the **Laboratoire Informatique d'Avignon (LIA)**, **Centre d'Enseignement et de Recherche en Informatique (CERI)**, **Avignon Université, France**.

I am a member of the **CORNET** research team, where I work under the supervision of **Prof. Yezekayel Hayel** on the **MUGS (Multi-layer Graph Games for Network Cyberdeception)** project funded by the **U.S. Army**.

My research focuses on developing mathematical models and computational methods for complex stochastic systems, with applications in communication networks, cyber deception, operations research, and artificial intelligence. My work combines rigorous theoretical analysis with algorithmic design in areas such as queueing systems, stochastic processes, reinforcement learning, and game theory.

Before joining Avignon Université, I was a **Postdoctoral Fellow** in the **Industrial Engineering and Operations Research** department at **IIT Bombay**, where I worked on stochastic games and reinforcement learning. I also visited **Inria Sophia Antipolis–Méditerranée** as a **Visiting Researcher**, collaborating with **Prof. Konstantin Avrachenkov** and the **NEO** research team.

I received my **Ph.D.** from the **National Institute of Technology Raipur, India**, where my research focused on advanced queueing systems and matrix-analytic methods.
